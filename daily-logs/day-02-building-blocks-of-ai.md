## 💡 Room Conclusion & Core Recap

- **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** The overarching field concerned with enabling machines to mimic human intelligence and reasoning.
- **Machine Learning (ML):** A subfield of AI where models learn from data to make predictions, following a structured lifecycle from data collection to deployment and monitoring.
- **ML Algorithms:** Fall into four categories—supervised, unsupervised, semi-supervised, and reinforcement learning—each suited to different problems and data types.
- **Neural Networks:** Replicate the structure of the human brain using layers of weighted nodes to extract complex features from raw input data.
- **Deep Learning:** Extends neural networks further using models with more than three layers, enabling self-learning at scale.
- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Built on transformer neural networks and trained on vast datasets to predict the next word in a sequence and generate human-like text, refined through RLHF.

## 📌 Task: Machine Learning Algorithms

### 🧠 Core Concepts & Structure
Machine Learning (ML) algorithms are mathematical methods used to learn patterns from data to produce **ML models**. Every ML algorithm follows a 3-step iterative process:
1. **Decision Process:** Makes predictions based on input data.
2. **Error Function:** Evaluates how far off those predictions were from the actual target.
3. **Model Optimization Process:** Adjusts the parameters of the algorithm to reduce error in future iterations.

---

### 📊 Categories of Machine Learning Algorithms

- **Supervised Learning:** Trains on **labelled data** (inputs come with explicit correct answers). Used for tasks like predicting prices or spam classification.
- **Unsupervised Learning:** Works with **unlabelled data** to discover hidden structures, clusters, or patterns on its own. Ideal for customer segmentation and anomaly detection.
- **Semi-Supervised Learning:** Combines a **small amount of labelled data** with a **large pool of unlabelled data** to guide learning cost-effectively.
- **Reinforcement Learning:** An agent learns by taking actions within an environment and receiving **rewards or penalties** based on the outcomes to maximize cumulative reward.
